From a zip to a link in 3 steps

  1. Drop the site’s files in

    Drag a folder, a zip or a single HTML file onto the page. It unzips in the tab and nothing is uploaded.

  2. Check the site in the preview

    Your files are served from browser storage, so relative links, images and scripts load as they would online.

  3. Share one version of the site

    Share uploads that one version and gives you two links: one that follows the live version and one pinned to it.

Does my site leave my computer?

Not until you press Share. Before that they stay in your browser’s storage, and the preview runs from there.

How big can a shared site be?

Up to 4 MB zipped, 50 MB unzipped and 2,000 files. Local previews are limited only by your browser’s storage.

Who can publish to my shared site?

Whoever holds its edit token. Site Runner creates it on the first share and keeps it in your browser; there are no accounts.

What if I clear my browser data?

Local sites and their versions go with it. Shared versions stay online, but you lose the token that publishes new ones.
